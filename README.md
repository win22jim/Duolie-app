# Duolie

Your table. Your rink.

Duolie is a completely free hockey shooting game for iPhone, built first for iPhone Duo tabletop play. Position the puck, flick toward the net, and outplay a reactive goalie. Play unlimited Quick Shoot, a ten-puck Shootout, or Target Practice.

No ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or upfront price. Offline play is available; Game Center is optional.

# Privacy Policy for Duolie

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026

Duolie is an iPhone hockey shooting game published by Jeremy Littlewood ("the developer"). This policy explains how the app and its support channels handle information.

## Summary

Duolie has no ads, tracking, purchases, developer-operated gameplay servers, or third-party analytics SDKs. You can play offline without creating a Duolie account. Personal stats and preferences are stored on your iPhone.

**Game Center is optional.** When you play while signed in, Duolie uses Apple's Game Center to send qualified scores and achievement progress and retrieve your Game Center progress. Apple handles that information under its own privacy terms. Information you voluntarily email or submit for support also reaches the developer. These exceptions are explained below.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Duolie saves information needed to run the game, including:

- Personal shot and goal totals, saves, accuracy, streaks, fastest-shot records, difficulty-specific totals, and the date you last played.
- Local Shootout and Target Practice records and game preferences.
- Difficulty, music and sound-effect levels, and haptic preferences.
- When Game Center is used, an app-specific Game Center player identifier and local score/achievement reporting records. These keep different players' progress separate and allow interrupted reports to be retried.
- Local technical diagnostics, such as the app version, display dimensions, safe areas, hinge configuration and recent input information, to help investigate layout or shooting problems.

The app does not automatically send local diagnostics to the developer. Settings provides an explicit option to share device diagnostics using the system share sheet; you choose whether and where to send them.

Duolie does not implement its own iCloud synchronization for personal stats. Your device's normal backup and restore settings may include app data, under Apple's terms.

## 2. Tilt and tabletop sensing

On regular iPhones, Duolie uses Apple's motion framework while the rink is active to determine the phone's angle and control puck lift. On iPhone Duo, it uses system-provided hinge and display-region information for tabletop layout and shot lift.

These readings are processed on your device for gameplay. Duolie does not use them to identify you, track your location, or build a fitness or activity history. Current layout or shot information can appear in local diagnostics or information you deliberately share for support. If regular-iPhone motion readings are unavailable, flat shots remain playable.

## 3. Optional Apple Game Center

Duolie uses Apple's GameKit framework for sign-in, leaderboards and achievements. An existing Game Center sign-in can be recognized automatically. You do not provide your Apple Account password to Duolie or the developer.

While authenticated, Duolie submits qualified goal totals and achievement progress to Apple and retrieves the signed-in player's existing progress. The app uses your app-specific Game Center player identifier locally and displays your Game Center display name. Gameplay does not require Game Center.

Scores and your Game Center identity may be visible to other players according to Apple's Game Center rules and your account's sharing settings. Apple also processes information about Game Center use under its own policy. For details and privacy controls, see [Apple Game Center & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/) and your device's Game Center settings.

Deleting Duolie does not delete scores or achievements already stored by Apple. Game Center account and service-data requests are handled through Apple.

## 4. Advertising, tracking and other permissions

Duolie does not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites. It includes no advertising, attribution, third-party analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

The app does not request your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photo library, calendars or health information. It does not record audio. Its instrumental music and effects are bundled with the app.

## 5. Support messages and diagnostics

If you email **rosters-taupe6p@icloud.com**, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond, troubleshoot issues and improve Duolie. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You may contact the same address to request deletion of correspondence the developer holds, subject to those obligations.

GitHub issues in this public repository are visible to everyone. Do not post private information there. GitHub handles website and account information under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Apple may make crash or diagnostic reports available through its developer services according to your device's analytics-sharing choices. TestFlight feedback you choose to submit is also provided through Apple. Duolie has no independent automatic telemetry service.

## 6. Retention, deletion and your choices

- You can play offline without signing in to Game Center.
- Manage Game Center sign-in and sharing through Apple's settings.
- Delete the app using **Delete App**, rather than Offload App, to remove its ordinary local app data from that device. Restoring a device backup may restore that data. Manage or delete backups separately through Apple.
- Game Center records are separate from local data and remain subject to Apple's controls and retention practices.
- You choose whether to send a support email, share diagnostics or post a public issue. Contact the developer about support information held by the developer or to exercise applicable privacy rights.

## 7. Children's privacy

Duolie does not use advertising or tracking for any player and does not require a separate account with the developer. Apple manages children's Game Center accounts, parental controls and activity-sharing rules. Parents and guardians should use those controls when enabling Game Center and avoid including a child's personal information in public support issues. Contact the developer if personal information about a child was sent to support and should be removed.

## 8. Security

Duolie relies on Apple's app sandbox and device security for local storage and Apple's services for Game Center authentication and reporting. The developer does not operate a separate account or gameplay database. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. Please report security concerns privately by email.

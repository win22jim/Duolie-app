# Duolie Support

Your table. Your rink. Duolie is a free hockey shooting game built first for iPhone Duo tabletop play, with a full-screen rink for regular iPhones.

## Getting started

### iPhone Duo tabletop

Place the lower display flat on a stable table and fold the goal display upright, around 90°. When iOS supplies the tabletop display regions, Duolie opens the rink automatically. Unfold to return home.

The lower display is your ice and puck. The upright display contains the net and goalie. Touch the puck and drag slowly to position it. To shoot, flick toward the net and release while your finger is moving. A faster flick sends a faster shot; the direction and position matter.

At 90°, shots stay low. Opening or closing away from 90° adds lift, using the angle when you release. For example, 45° and 135° produce matching lift. Larger angle changes can send the puck over the net.

### Regular iPhone

Choose a mode on the home screen and tap **Take the ice**. Hold the screen face up for a flat shot. Tilt the phone away from flat to add lift when you release. Use the Home control on the rink to return to the main screen.

If motion readings are unavailable, you can still play using flat shots. Regular-iPhone tilt is separate from Duo's hinge-based control.

## Game modes and difficulty

- **Quick Shoot:** unlimited shots to practice and score.
- **Shootout:** ten attempts; make them count, then try a rematch.
- **Target Practice:** aim for the lit corner targets across ten shots.
- **Open Net:** practice without a goalie.
- **Rookie / Pro:** reactive goalies, with Pro offering the stronger challenge. Position the puck, change direction, and try to create an opening before releasing.

Mode and difficulty controls are available from home and Settings. Some choices are locked during a shot or an active challenge round; finish the shot/round, start a rematch or return to Quick Shoot to change them.

## Music, effects and haptics

Settings has separate controls for music and sound effects, including independent volume levels and on/off switches. Haptics can be turned off separately. Device hardware and system settings affect haptic availability. Sound may play in Silent Mode when enabled in Duolie; use the in-app controls to mute it.

## Game Center and personal stats

Open **Leaderboards** or **Achievements** from home to connect to Apple's Game Center. Game Center is optional; internet access and a signed-in Apple Account are needed for online features.

Duolie has separate Rookie and Pro lifetime-goal leaderboards and twelve achievements. Only qualifying live play earns online credit. Open Net practice and replay playback do not count toward rankings. Personal stats include completed live shots and are saved on this iPhone; local Shootout and Target Practice bests are also tracked.

Weekly leaderboards, friend challenges and multiplayer are not included in version 1.0.

**My online progress has not updated.** Check your internet connection and Game Center sign-in, then reopen Duolie and the relevant Game Center screen. Interrupted reports can retry later. Check that you were using an eligible goalie difficulty. Local stats and online totals have different eligibility rules and need not match.

**Will my stats sync to another iPhone?** Local personal stats and round records do not have Duolie-managed cross-device sync. Game Center maintains the online records associated with your Apple account. Deleting the app removes local app data; Apple-hosted scores and achievements are separate. See the [Privacy Policy](duolie-privacy-policy.md).

## Troubleshooting

**Duo stays on the home screen when folded.** Use portrait orientation, put the lower display flat, and adjust the upper display near 90°. Try unfolding and folding again. Duolie relies on the actual tabletop regions supplied by iOS. If it still does not enter the rink, send the app/build number, iOS version and device diagnostics by email.

**The puck moves but a flick does not shoot.** Make sure the game is not paused. A slow drag positions the puck; release during a forward flick toward the goal to shoot. If a clear flick repeatedly fails, report where the gesture started and ended, whether the puck stayed visible, and the app build. A recording is especially helpful.

**Shots always stay low on a regular iPhone.** Check for a tilt-unavailable message. If available, allow Motion & Fitness access for Duolie in iOS Settings. Hold the display face up first, then tilt while playing. Flat-shot play remains available if the system cannot supply readings.

**No sound or haptics.** Check Duolie's separate music/effects switches and levels, device output volume and audio route, and the Haptics switch. Haptics require compatible hardware and are not reproduced by every simulator.

**The app stops responding.** Close and reopen it, then check for an app update. Please email reproducible problems before deleting the app, because deletion removes local stats and preferences.

## Privacy and cost

Duolie is completely free, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Offline gameplay needs no account. Game Center is Apple's optional online service. Read the [Privacy Policy](duolie-privacy-policy.md) for local storage, motion, Game Center and support-message details.

## System requirements

- **Regular iPhone:** iOS 26.0 or later.
- **iPhone Duo native tabletop play:** iOS 27.1 or later and an active system tabletop configuration.
- **Game Center:** optional Apple Account sign-in and connectivity.
